List of properties, and examples of use, that can be used in file ".properties" of Optifine/ETF/ETM Resource Packs
All properties that is not boolean, support list values, for example: "modLoaded.25=sodium iris jade".

- angry (angry.1=true)                                                                        [ETF]
- baby (baby.1=true)                                                                          [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- biomeTag (biomeTag.1=is_river OR biomeTag.1=magic_mod:magical_biomes)                       [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- biomes (biomes.1=plains OR biomes.1=biome_mod:that_biome)                                   [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- blockAbove (blockAbove.1=oak_leaves OR blockAbove.1=block_mod:block)                        [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- blockAboveSolid (blockAboveSolid.1=stone OR blockAboveSolid.1=block_mod:block)              [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- blockBelow (blockBelow.1=oak_leaves OR blockBelow.1=block_mod:block)                        [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- blockBelowSolid (blockBelowSolid.1=stone OR blockBelowSolid.1=block_mod:block)              [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- blockSpawned (blockSpawned.1=stone OR blockSpawned.1=block_mod:block)                       [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- clientGameMode (clientGameMode.1=0 --- 0=Surv.,1=Crea.,2=Adv.,3=Spect)                      [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- colors (colors.1=magenta --- refers to wolf/cat collar and sheep/lamma color)             [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- creeperCharged (creeperCharged.1=true)                                                      [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- dayTime (dayTime.1=2000-10500 --- time in ticks 0-24000)                                    [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- dimension (dimension.1=overworld --- overworld,the_nether,the_end)                          [ETF]
- distance (distance.1=5-20 --- distance in blocks from player)                               [ETF]
- hardcore (hardcore.1=true --- if world is in hardcore)                                      [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- health (health.1=5-8 OR health.1=25-60% --- health from mob)                                [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- heights (heights.1=(-40)-30 OR heights.1=20-60 --- height in Y Coordinate)                  [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- hiddenGene (hiddenGene.1=lazy --- normal,lazy,worried,playfull,brown,weak,agressive)      [ETF]
- hour (hour.1=0-14 --- hour of the day 0-24)                                                 [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- isClientPlayer (isClientPlayer.1=true --- if the mob is the Client player)                  [ETF]
- isCreative (isCreative.1=true --- if the player is in creative)                             [ETF]
- isSpawner (isSpawner.1=true --- if the mob is a miniature inside a spawner cage)            [ETF]
- isTeammate (isTeammate.1=true --- if the mob is a teammate or player teammate)              [ETF]
- items (items.1=minecraft:dirt --- list,none,any,holding,wearing --- "any" is holding or     [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
  wearing any item, "wearing" is wearing any item, "none" is mob not holding or
  wearing nothing)
- jumpStrength (jumpStrength.1=0.4-1.0 --- applicable for horses, mules and donkeys)          [ETF]
- language (language.1=en_us --- language of game client)                                     [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- light (light.1=0-7 --- light level where the mob is in)                                     [ETF]
- llamaInventory (llamaInventory.1=2-4 --- inventory slots of the llama)                      [ETF]
- maxHealth (maxHealth.1=15-30 --- applicable for all mobs)                                   [ETF]
- speed (speed.1=0.1125-0.3375 --- applicable for all mobs)                                   [ETF]
- minecraftVersion (minecraftVersion.1=1.18.2 --- game client version)                        [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- minute (minute.1=0-30 --- the minute of the hour 0-59)                                      [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- modLoaded (modLoaded.1=sodium)                                                              [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- modelSuffix (modelSuffix.1=2 --- check if the entity is using the desired model variant)    [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- month (month.1=1-6 --- the month of the year 0-11)                                          [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- monthDay (monthDay.1=1-15 --- the day of the month 0-31)                                    [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- moonPhase (moonPhase.1=2 OR moonPhase.1=2-5 --- the moon phase 0-7)                         [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- moving (moving.1=true --- if the mob is horizontally moving)                                [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- name (name.1="Talon" --- check the entitie name --- name_list,regex,pattern --- if starting [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
  with "!" perform negative check. If the string have only "0-9 A-Z a-z _ - . +" characters,
  the double quotes or single quotes is not required. is allowed to use regex and patterns
  like the examples:
  - regex:\\d+                                  (regex)
  - regex:\\\\                                  (regex)
  - /\\/\\                                      (escaping backslashes)
  - pattern:Pinky*                              (* is a wildcard)
  - ipattern:Pinky*                             (* with "ipattern" is insensitive)
  - regex:Pin(k)+y                              (regex)
  - regex:(birch golem|.* g1)                   (regex)
  - iregex:Pin(k)+y                             (insensitive regex)
  - iregex:stormy|stormy chicken|7              (regex)
  Examples of string:
  - "All your base are belong to us"
  - '{looks:"like something fancy",but:"alas, this is just a string!"}'
  - "Oh hello, \"Jeanine\"..."
  - 'My reflection \"winked\" at me. I covered the mirror in the attic just to be safe.'
  - "Documents\\Folder")
- nbt (nbt.1.nbtPath=value --- check if match with the informed NBT                           [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
  - "nbtPath" must match the exact name of the nbt element and is case-sensitive
  - "nbtPath" supports matching nested nbt elements by defining the full nbt path with
    the '.' marking separation, e.g., the Villager profession level is 'VillagerData.level'
    meaning the full property key would be "nbt.1.VillagerData.level"
  - to check if the value of nbt matches, is used string, 
    e.g., "nbt.1.carriedBlockState.Name=minecraft:grass_block" or
    "nbt.1.CustomName='pattern:Pinky*'" or "nbt.1.ArmorItems.0.id=exists:false"
  - all string syntax showed in the "name" propery, described above, is valid for the
    property "nbt" too
  - All these start strings is allowed:
    exits:,raw:,range:,regex:,iregex:,pattern:,ipattern:,string_list
  - use the command "/data get" to read all NBT data of a entity, to use as basis)
- nbtClient (nbt.1.nbtPath=value --- all described in "nbt" property, but valid for           [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
  the player Client)
- nbtVehicle (nbt.1.nbtPath=value --- all described in "nbt" property, but valid for          [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
  ridden entity. this will return "false" if the mob is not riding any entity)
- playerCreated (playerCreated.1=true --- if the player was created the golem)                [ETF]
- professions (profession.1=minecraft:farmer:1 OR profession.1=minecraft:farmer:2-4 OR        [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
  profession.1=minecraft:farmer
  - List of professions
  - none,minecraft:armorer,minecraft:butcher,minecraft:cartographer,minecraft:cleric,
    minecraft:farmer,minecraft:fisherman,minecraft:fletcher,minecraft:leatherworker,
    minecraft:librarian,minecraft:mason,minecraft:nitwit,minecraft:shepherd,
    minecraft:toolsmith,minecraft:weaponsmith)
- screamingGoat (screamingGoat.1=true --- if the goat is screamming)                          [ETF]
- second (second.1=0-25 --- seconds of minute 0-59)                                           [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- sizes (sizes.1=3 OR sizes.1=0-2 --- only valid for mobs with sizes 0=smal,1=medium,         [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
  3=big)
- temperature (temperature.1=0.2-0.25 --- temp. of spawn, driven by biome and world height)   [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- textureSuffix (textureSuffix.1=2 same for "modelSuffix" property, but valid for textures)   [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- weather (weather.1=rain --- check weather using clear,rain,thunder)                         [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- weekDay (weekDay.1=1-4 --- day of the week 1-7 where 1=sunday and 7=saturday)               [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- year (year.1=2020-2022 --- the year 0-9999)                                                 [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
- yearDay (yearDay.1=35-50 --- day of the year 1-366)                                         [Optifine/ETF/ETM]
